# Quiet as a Mouse V1.3 - Animal design review

All 210 roster animals have individual design notes below. These notes describe
the identifying features used for the cartoon illustrations and link to the
reference material. Broad animal names use a representative form; domestic
breeds, sexes, ages, and individuals may look different. Small details and
real-world size differences are simplified for visibility in the game.

Movement labels describe the animation used in this game, not every behavior
the animal can perform. Game rarity is unchanged from V1.2.1 and is not a
current wildlife-population estimate.

Use animal-preview.html to inspect the illustrations and animations directly.
The designs and poses were rendered for visual review; live-browser and
physical-device testing was not available in this run.

Slender segmented abdomen, long proboscis and long fine legs; One pair of narrow translucent wings; legs move only slightly while flying.

Pale wingless worker with a broad waist rather than an ant's petiole; Straight beaded antennae and six short legs.

Pear-shaped green abdomen with a paired set of rear cornicles; Small head, fine antennae and six slender legs; no wings on this adult form.
